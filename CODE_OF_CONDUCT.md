# Code of conduct

Tempo is a small project and should be a pleasant place for everyone who uses it or helps
with it.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are volunteers, and English is not everyone's first
  language.
- Talk about the code, the design or the idea. Never about the person.
- Assume good intent, and say so plainly when something is not working for you.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and that a "no" is about the change.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminating against anyone for who they are or where they come from.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.

## If something goes wrong

Use the "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer privately
through their GitHub profile ([@saikatnjm](https://github.com/saikatnjm)). Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that
break these rules.

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests and any other place where the project is
discussed.
